## Lorenzo aka Paraguay Voyager

Lorenzo is a friend from Veneto who used to run Hostal Mediterráneo, listed on [Booking](https://www.booking.com/Share-i1qCzoH?) and [Hostelworld](https://www.hostelworld.com/hostels/p/326334/mediterraneo-hostal/), in downtown Asunción — now run by another Italian friend.  

Lorenzo has long handled temporary and permanent residencies, including for digital nomads, through [Paraguay Voyager](https://www.paraguayenjoyer.com?utm_source=g.lpy.lat). Following recent, small-but-important reforms, he now offers residency and cédula for USD 1,400 in a single trip.
